# NF-003 Usability Study Plan — Online Internship & Placement Preparation Portal

**Status: planned, not executed.** This document prepares the study; NF-003 must remain open until sessions are conducted and findings are recorded.

## Objective

Evaluate whether intended portal users can complete representative student and recruiter tasks using the current integrated application, with particular attention to navigation clarity, form labels, feedback, error recovery and responsive layout.

## Participants and setup

- Recruit 5 student participants who resemble the portal's intended student users. Add 2 recruiter participants for recruiter-only flows if recruiter usability is in this sprint's agreed scope.
- Use a non-production deployment and test accounts with synthetic data.
- Test at desktop width (approximately 1366 × 768) and mobile width (approximately 390 × 844).
- Ask participants to think aloud. Do not coach them through the interface; provide help only after recording that they are blocked.
- Obtain consent before recording notes or screen activity. Do not collect real passwords, resumes or personal information.

## Student tasks

1. Sign in and locate an internship with a stated deadline. Explain how you decide whether it is open.
2. Open an internship and find what is required before applying.
3. Locate aptitude practice, start an available assessment, select an answer, change it, and submit.
4. Find the submitted score and review which answers were correct.
5. Open preparation resources, filter to technical preparation, open one resource, and mark it completed.
6. Return to resources after a refresh and show where completion status is displayed.

## Recruiter tasks (when included)

1. Locate your own listings.
2. Create or edit a listing and identify any validation feedback.
3. Open an application and update its status.

## Measures to record

For each task, record: success without help / success with help / failure; completion time; navigation errors; validation or recovery problems; participant comments; and observed accessibility issues. Note the viewport size and browser.

After tasks, ask participants to rate overall ease of use from 1 (very difficult) to 5 (very easy), then collect the three most confusing and three most helpful parts of the experience. Do not claim a standardized SUS score unless the full validated questionnaire is administered and scored correctly.

## Suggested acceptance criteria (confirm with Product Owner before the study)

- At least 4 of 5 student participants complete each core student task without moderator help.
- No participant can access another student's assessment results or saved resource progress.
- No critical usability blocker prevents starting/submitting an assessment or applying for an internship.
- Any repeated navigation or comprehension issue is converted into a defect or backlog item with evidence.
